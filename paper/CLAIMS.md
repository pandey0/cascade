# Claim -> evidence map (update whenever a claim changes)
| Claim | Status | Evidence (file) | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Naive empirical-threshold selection misses its target in about half of calibration draws near the achievable risk | supported on Banking77, CLINC150 (gold teacher), synthetic | `results/exp1a_*.json`, `results/exp0_*.json`, Fig. 1 | misses are small; gold labels as teacher |
| Valid (Bonferroni/Holm) selection holds violations below delta | supported (observed 0-0.5%) | same | i.i.d. calibration; very conservative; Holm gains <= ~1 point over Bonferroni |
| Uncorrected Wilson margin stayed near the nominal level on these tasks | observed (4.5-10% at m>=500), NOT a guarantee | Fig. 1, `results/SUMMARY.md` | benign i.i.d. tasks; fails at small m (12% at CLINC150 m=250); untested under shift |
| Cost of validity vs naive is about 4-12 coverage points at 1,000-2,000 audit labels on the real tasks (much larger at m=500 on CLINC150 and on the synthetic task) | supported | Fig. 2, `results/SUMMARY.md` | task dependent |
| Strict-start fixed-sequence selection almost never deploys | supported | `evidence/diagnostics/strict_sequence_pitfall.csv`, Fig. 4 | design flaw, not a theorem |
| TRACER's final certification is one-shot and all-or-nothing | read in code and changelog; modelled as `certify_once` | `docs/notes/tracer-analysis.md`, TRACER `fit/certification.py` | not run; not a reproduction |
| With a real LLM teacher (Claude Haiku 5.5: 76.5% / 90.9% agreement with gold on Banking77 / CLINC150), Bonferroni/Holm still keep violations of the *teacher-agreement* target <= 0.1% while naive misses 30-55% | supported (3 splits, m=400) | `results/exp1b_*_vs_teacher.json` | one teacher, one prompt |
| Agreement with a noisy teacher is neither sufficient nor necessary for correctness: distilled students violate the gold-error target (Banking77 alpha=0.10: 70% of draws) while gold-trained students beat the teacher (92% vs 76%) and a teacher-agreement gate under-deploys them | supported | `results/exp1b_*_vs_gold.json`, `docs/notes/exp1b-findings.md` | pools of 1000; gold labels have own noise |
| Under mild shift, rolling Holm recalibration on uniformly audited labels cuts block-level violations vs static Holm (39-65% -> 2-33% on Banking77) at lower coverage; rolling naive is worse than static | supported (preliminary) | `results/exp2_*.json`, `docs/notes/exp2-findings.md` | one student/shift instance; synthetic shifts |
| Under severe shift (unseen classes) no policy restores the target within 6,000 decisions at <= 10% audit | observed | same | horizon and window dependent |
| Anytime-valid CS alarm had no pre-shift false alarms; a re-evaluated z-test had up to 22% | observed | same | CS detects late/rarely at low audit rates |
| Break-even is on the order of 5,000-12,500 decisions for 3,000 labels at 30-60% coverage (teacher cost measured at $0.0002/call) | model output, not measured savings | `results/economics.md`, `src/cascade/economics.py` | excludes engineering time and error costs; coverage values illustrative |
| Micro-batching: with pinned cores and no other load, 3 quiet paired runs gave 1.41-1.58x throughput and ~35% lower p50 (24 clients); two further pairs were contaminated by my own concurrent checks; earlier unpinned sets mixed | observed, noisy host; an earlier unpaired run showed the opposite | `docs/perf.md`, `evidence/logs/perf_ab.jsonl` | contended host; not capacity numbers |
